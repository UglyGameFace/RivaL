# Automatic shadow evaluation (no trading)

After Discloud deploys a passing commit, RivaL's existing PostgreSQL
perpetual collector continues to run as before. A separate, low-priority
background task now reevaluates locally stored market history once per hour
(`RIVAL_SHADOW_EVAL_SECONDS`, allowed 900–21600). Set
`RIVAL_SHADOW_LEARNING_ENABLED=false` to disable shadow work while leaving
live market collection and Discord functioning.

- No exchange requests, Gemini requests, account credentials, leverage,
  trade execution or Discord messages are generated by this worker.
- At most ten distinct, recently collecting market streams per cycle; least
  recently evaluated streams are served first across bot restarts.
- The worker queries at most 1,000 *already stored* 5-minute candles per
  stream and processes 5-minute and 15-minute prediction horizons. Only
  source observations first recorded before the as-of time are visible.
- Training is online-in-sequence and strictly uses resolved prior outcomes.
  Backfilled data discovered at once cannot qualify as genuine forward
  evaluation evidence. Source data with invalid timing is skipped.
- The independent PostgreSQL table `perp_shadow_evaluations` stores sample
  counts, settled evaluations, proper Brier scores, a base-rate baseline and
  whether a *shadow candidate* beat its baseline. Updates are idempotent per
  venue/contract/quote/horizon/model version. No model checkpoints are
  promoted or used for live long/short calls.
- `/rival-research` -> **Math & History** displays aggregated,
  settled-outcome metrics only after 50 evaluated outcomes per stream.
  Metrics from overlapping venues and horizons must not be treated as
  independent market accuracy. The menu explicitly distinguishes shadow
  evidence from validated deployable signals.
- If a cycle fails, it logs a sanitized failure and tries again later.
  Discord and the market collector keep running. The worker is cancelled
  and joined on shutdown, before PostgreSQL closes.

This is *automatic recurring shadow retraining/evaluation*, not the
production self-learning forecasting system requested in issue #6.
Still required: backfill/quality audits, reliable reconciliation of actual
outcomes, independent holdout/calibration, versioned approved model registry,
multi-venue prediction generation and real-world performance checks.
A successful Github Actions run does not prove that Discloud actually ran
the worker; inspect deployed logs (`RivaL automatic shadow learning`) and
the research panel for production evidence.

## Prospective shadow-model governance hardening

A **qualified_shadow** label is not permission to bet. The governance
evaluator now includes only actual `prospective-logistic-shadow-v1` records
with settled outcomes. It groups all venues at one UTC target time as
**one correlated event** and computes paired, per-target model-versus-baseline
Brier improvement.

Qualification requires 100 or more distinct targets across at least seven
calendar days, average paired gain strictly greater than 0.005, positive
average gain on at least 80% of those days, and a positive daily block
stress margin (daily mean minus 2.58 standard errors). The margin is an
**intentionally conservative heuristic**, not an independently validated
95% confidence interval or proof of an exploitable trading edge. Thin,
noisy, contradictory or drifting evidence must stay `insufficient` or
`rejected_shadow`. The evidence/gain is written into governance reasons
and can requalify/reject as fresh targets settle. Invalid scores and model
versions are not mixed into the vote.

The Accuracy tab's seven-day trend now compares the model's
`baseline_brier - model_brier` skill in each period. An improving raw
Brier score on an easier week is not presented as learning progress if the
baseline improved even faster. All performance is *observed shadow research*
without actual fills, funding, slippage or verified net profitability.

## Prospective regime proof, prediction ledger and execution-cost gate

The existing five-minute background process issues research forecasts before
future closes and settles them later from immutable source-specific candles.
RivaL now also timestamps pre-outcome quiet, normal or turbulent market
regimes using five-minute historical return volatility observed at issuance.
The fingerprints of the actual features and model weights used are stored
alongside the original prospective forecast in the same DB transaction.
This is a provenance audit, not a pre-trained accuracy claim.

Accuracy -> **Prediction ledger** lists the newest real recorded 5m/15m
forecasts, their original P(up), issuance time and pending/settled status.
Accuracy -> **Market-regime skill** compares paired forward Brier against a
baseline across unique UTC target windows, excluding disputed regime labels
and withholding stable-skill status until at least two distinct conditions,
50 future targets and 7 days in each, with positive skill across at least
two later sampled weeks. The thresholds are research heuristics, not
statistical proof of a profitable edge. Underperformance in a regime remains
visible, not averaged away.

Accuracy -> **Actual cost proof** is deliberately blocked. Public OHLCV
candles and hypothetical fee/slippage/funding stress do not contain account-
specific entry/exit fills or confirmed paid fees and funding. Without
read-only authenticated matching trade receipts, contemporaneous arrival
bid/ask benchmarks and reconciled funding payments, RivaL must **never**
label the strategy profitable, even if forward directional forecasts become
more accurate. No orders or trades are authorized or placed by these checks.
Once execution evidence is available through an authorized account, that
is a separate audited connector and reconciliation implementation.

Existing past predictions remain available but are **not** retroactively
assigned a market regime using future information. New records accumulate as
the live bot runs. All data freshness is tied to PostgreSQL, not to a
cached Discord screenshot. The old model remains under its no-trade gate.
