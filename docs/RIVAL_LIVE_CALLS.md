# Live research calls for the selected market

RivaL's Discord **/rival → Markets → select an exchange/Polymarket/Kalshi market → Post Live Calls**, or **Tracking → Post Live Calls** after selecting a market, posts one restart-persistent card to the server channel. The controls **Refresh** and **Stop** are author-restricted; **/rival-stop** and **Stop All** revoke every panel owned by that Discord user. The bot never posts unsolicited calls to a public channel, never falls back to DMs, and has a global bound of 24 cards and four per owner.

- **Exchange:** One card for BTC, ETH, SOL, or XRP with *actual* 5-, 15-, and 60-minute forward research forecasts. Each UP/DOWN direction shows a numeric confidence score, its model, original source, issued time, and target expiration. Unqualified models remain visible but are marked **NO TRADE CALL**, and the figure is the raw issued model estimate unless the pinned snapshot explicitly records a passed calibration gate. Where another target has **already been prospectively issued for the same source**, the card displays its target window, direction, and confidence next to the current forecast. If the next forecast does not yet exist, the card says so.
- **Polymarket:** Uses the *same* official Gamma/CLOB current/next contract board and frozen contract-specific model snapshot as RivaL's original tracked Polymarket panel. It adds raw frozen call confidence for the verified current contract; the next contract's listed price-to-beat/open/close data are separate from any next-call prediction, which is absent until actually issued. Does not convert a futures candle into a Polymarket strike or pretend to have verified settlement odds.
- **Kalshi:** Uses independently listed and quoted crypto contracts with their actual titles and closing times. Shows YES/NO and raw model confidence only when the same contract's previously frozen Kalshi forward prediction can be read. Does not fabricate 5/15/60-minute contracts or a Bitcoin UP/DOWN from an unrelated Kalshi threshold.
- **DraftKings:** An exchange-specific automated research call cannot be proven from current CDNA public/personal reference feeds. The button declines to post rather than borrowing forecasts, fabricating contract odds, or exposing another user's personal feed.

**Freshness:** The exchange panel is woken by a genuine new 5-minute forecast cycle and also checks for a newly persisted issuance every **15 seconds**. The expensive model render is bounded to at most once per **60 seconds** in the absence of issuance; the card is edited only if its actual fingerprint changes. Polymarket contract checks are bounded to **30 seconds**; Kalshi catalog checks to **45 seconds** and use its native 35-second cache. Actual provider/DB latency can delay these intervals, so these are scheduling targets rather than hard delivery guarantees. Discord messages are edited in place. No historical backfill or synthesized next target ever becomes a new live call.

**Entry, stop, TP1/TP2, risk/reward:** These are always marked *not verified* unless a market-specific, actually calculated, authenticated target model exists. A live quote, a contract strike, a directional probability, and a user-entered price alert are not equivalent to a validated execution plan. No orders are placed.

**Reliability:** The new panel is deliberately independent of the retired automatic DM Auto-Hunt producer and does not revive it. It reuses the existing persisted prediction, frozen contract data and forecast worker. The exchange issuance wake does not trigger extraneous Gamma/CLOB/Kalshi requests. Failure of one market, channel, or model does not stop the others. Existing profitability and promotion gates are unchanged.
